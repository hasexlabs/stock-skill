## Scoring anchors

### Strategic importance

- **0–2:** Single narrow application, discretionary demand, or unclear customer value.
- **3–5:** Useful product in one credible market with limited adjacency.
- **6–8:** Enabling infrastructure for multiple growing markets or a hard bottleneck.
- **9–10:** Mission-critical layer with broad structural demand and strong customer urgency.

### Moat / defensibility

- **0–2:** Commodity, easily substituted, or no evidence of customer preference.
- **3–5:** Some relationships, service, or execution advantage but easy incumbent response.
- **6–8:** Verified IP, qualification, installed base, manufacturing, switching, or workflow integration.
- **9–10:** Several reinforcing barriers with measurable performance and long replacement cycles.

### Founder / insider quality

- **0–2:** No evidence of alignment, poor governance, or repeated value destruction.
- **3–5:** Some insider ownership or stable leadership, but no verified founder/technical link.
- **6–8:** Long-term owner-manager or meaningful technical founder involvement with credible behavior.
- **9–10:** Verified founder control or ownership plus strong technical leadership and disciplined capital allocation.

### Economics / capital efficiency

- **0–2:** Pre-revenue, severe cash burn, high capex, or chronic dilution.
- **3–5:** Revenue exists but weak margins, cash conversion, or operating leverage.
- **6–8:** Attractive gross margin, manageable capex, positive or improving cash flow, and credible leverage.
- **9–10:** Strong cash economics, high returns on incremental capital, recurring revenue, and durable pricing power.

### Valuation

- **0–2:** Price assumes near-perfect execution or lacks a defensible denominator.
- **3–5:** Fairly valued for current quality; limited margin of safety.
- **6–8:** Reasonable or attractive on cash-adjusted EV, earnings, FCF, or conservative scenarios.
- **9–10:** Clearly low expectations plus strong current economics and multiple ways to win.

### Survivability / balance sheet

- **0–2:** Short runway, heavy debt, financing dependence, or fragile customers.
- **3–5:** Adequate liquidity but meaningful cyclical, concentration, or dilution risk.
- **6–8:** Solid cash position, manageable debt, and capacity to fund the plan.
- **9–10:** Net cash, strong cash generation, diversified customers, and high downside resilience.
