# Source hierarchy for picks-and-shovels research

Use sources in this order and explain exceptions:

1. **Regulatory filings:** SEC 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, proxy, equivalent foreign filings, and XBRL data for revenue, margins, cash, debt, shares, risk factors, management, and ownership.
2. **Official company materials:** investor-relations pages, earnings releases, technical datasheets, product pages, patents, presentations, and conference-call transcripts.
3. **Government and technical sources:** procurement records, standards bodies, research laboratories, academic papers, and independent test reports.
4. **Customer and partner sources:** customer announcements, supplier agreements, deployment records, and named case studies.
5. **Reputable financial and trade media:** discovery, context, market mapping, and triangulation.
6. **Aggregators and search snippets:** discovery only; do not use as the sole support for material claims.

## Minimum evidence checklist

For every finalist verify: legal identity and listing; fiscal year and currency; product and payer; revenue and growth; gross and operating margin; cash, debt, dilution and cash runway; management and founder/insider alignment; competitors and substitutes; product qualification or switching costs; and the specific evidence supporting any market-size or newer-method claim.

## Derived-metric conventions

Market cap = latest share price × latest disclosed diluted or basic shares, with the choice labeled. EV = market cap + debt − cash; state whether leases are excluded. Trailing figures must combine comparable fiscal periods only. Separate reported figures from estimates and scenario outputs. Attach a date to every market-data observation.
